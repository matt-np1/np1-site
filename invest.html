The frontier

Anyone will be able to build an app next year. Very few will own the farm, the factory or the coffee shop it makes better.

The market

Tech has the biggest growth ceiling, and we respect that. As AI makes software cheap and universal, the edge moves to whoever owns the real operation, its customers and its data, and knows where tech pays. A farm run on great systems will outstrip the agri software sold to it.

The people

A generation of capable first-generation founders will start businesses anyway. Back them first and you own the early stake in that wave.

How the studio makes money

Probability over amplitude

Many businesses that each grow steadily and pay distributions, rather than one unicorn carrying the fund.

Discovery instead of diligence

Working inside a business reveals the founder, the economics and the risks, so follow-on capital goes into the best companies before they are re-rated.

Camels, not unicorns

Understandable demand, sound margins and modest capital needs make success possible at many sizes.

Compounding capability

Every venture adds systems, people and judgement that make the next one faster and cheaper to build.
