What, proof, opportunity

WhatControlled-environment farms, built to one standard and run by local teams on one operating system: training, a farm app and data, back office and market access.
ProofDullstroom has traded for four years and is profitable at farm level, run by a local team of six trained on site.
OpportunityBlack farmers hold an estimated 5 to 10% of commercial production. A farm that is stable from day one gives first-generation farmers a real route into ownership, and gives capital a repeatable unit.

The farm team in the grow tunnel, Dullstroom.

How it works

Ezulwini designs, builds and commissions each farm, then runs every farm on the same system, backed by trainers and specialists from the Hekpoort hub. Each new farm is co-owned with its farmer, who works towards 60%, and pays head office a R1.2m setup margin once and a 6.5% service fee annually.
