How our investment works

South Africa copied a startup playbook written for people with rich uncles.

Most businesses in the US and Europe start with money from friends and family. Most South African founders don't have that round, or a network, or someone who has done it before.

A new business then spends two to four years finding structure and profitability before it is bankable. Development programmes do valuable work in that stretch, preparing founders through training and technical assistance. Investors arrive once a business has proof. Building the business and investing in it from the start is still open, and that is where ownership is cheapest.
