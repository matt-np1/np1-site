About

Conviction, tested by doing.

Matt Beckett and Mitch Marescia started N+1 on a simple conviction: capable people and practical opportunities deserve a better route into business ownership.

Our story

From research to a portfolio

We started out wanting to help build 100 businesses, each with 10 good jobs. The research showed us what that takes, and it gave us our name. N+1 means favouring the system over the goal: add one more business, one more productive person, and keep building well past a hundred.

Early-stage investing commonly expects about 1 in 10 businesses to win. We aim for 9 in 10 that thrive. 4 in 7 is our honest starting point. The failures were cheap in capital and expensive in time.

What the pilot taught us

Six lessons from three years inside the businesses

Capacity comes first

A founder cannot coordinate scattered specialists into a management team. Businesses need a team with the time and incentive to make progress.

Build the team in-house

Outsourced partners could not give the companies everything they needed. That is why the Engine is an in-house team.

A plan for each stage

Development, launch and growth need their own timeframes, accountable people and realistic budgets.

Work done beats advice

Founders gain most from services and management, with advice second.

Care for the relationship

Expectations, trust and clear responsibilities shape everything. The founder owns operations.

Discovery beats diligence

Working inside a business reveals more than any pitch or remote assessment.
