01

What, proof, opportunity

WhatUniformitys provides school uniforms where parents live and work in townships. Moving towards local manufacture, bringing pride to kasi schools.
ProofFour schools are fully on board and we have sold to a handful of others. The next 5 or 6 are ready for a full ramp-up, and 23 schools have expressed real intent to make us their supplier. Schools invite us to parent meetings and give us space to sell on their grounds.
OpportunityNo one supplies these schools locally. Parents travel up to 40 km by taxi to town to buy generic uniforms and try to match colours and badges themselves. A school of 1,000 learners is worth about R1m per year to a local maker, and new schools keep opening.
02

Why N+1 backed it

Sphiwe Masinga grew up in Kabokweni and saw a simple product his schools needed that nobody supplied. N+1: Impact Venture Partners put in R120k at the end of 2024 so he could buy stock and leave his call-centre job, and has supported him since with strategy, accounts, brand and design.

Four schools are now fully on board and the next 5 or 6 are ready for a full ramp-up. What holds Uniformitys back is supply: bought-in stock from Johannesburg takes up to three months.
