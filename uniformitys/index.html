Capital raise · November 2026

Dressed as one. Made close to home.

Uniformitys makes and sells school uniforms in Kabokweni, Mpumalanga. The raise builds its own factory and shop, so it can supply every learner at the schools already asking for it.

R500k → R1.5m → R5mRaising, in three tranches
2028Cash positive, no further raise planned
16Schools fully served by 2031, up from 4 today

At a glance

Demand is proven. Supply is the constraint.

Four schools are fully on board and 23 more have expressed real intent, but Uniformitys can supply only about 10% of their learners while stock comes from Johannesburg on a three-month lead time. The raise builds the local capacity to close that gap.
